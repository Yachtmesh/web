Support

Need a hand?

Yachtmesh is open source, so most support happens out in the open on GitHub — bug reports, feature requests, and questions from the community all live there.

🐛

Found a bug?

Open an issue on the relevant Yachtmesh repository with as much detail as you can — steps to reproduce, hardware involved, and any logs help us track it down faster.

Open an issue →
💡

Have an idea?

Feature requests and discussions are welcome. Browse existing issues first to see if it's already being tracked, then add your voice or open a new one.

Browse GitHub →
✉️

Something else?

For account, order, or press-related questions — anything that isn't a GitHub issue — email us directly and we'll get back to you.

support@yachtmesh.io
🧭

Not sure where to start?

Check the waitlist and contact form on the homepage, or reach out on social — we're active and happy to point you in the right direction.

Go to contact form →

We're a small, heads-down team building toward early access, so response times can vary — but we read everything.
