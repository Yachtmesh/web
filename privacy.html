Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 July 2026

Who we are

Yachtmesh is an unincorporated project based in the Netherlands. This policy explains what information we collect through yachtmesh.io, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Waitlist sign-up: your email address, so we can let you know when early access opens.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, and the message you send us, so we can reply to you.
  • Anti-abuse checks: your IP address, used briefly to enforce a rate limit and verify the Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA on our forms, so we can stop spam and automated abuse. IP addresses are not stored beyond what's needed for this check.

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies, and we don't track you across other sites.

How we use your information

  • To respond to messages sent through the contact form.
  • To notify waitlist subscribers when early access becomes available.
  • To protect our forms from spam and automated abuse.

We will never sell your information, and we send waitlist emails only when there's something meaningful to share.

Third parties we use

We rely on a small number of service providers to run this site and its forms:

  • Loops.so — stores waitlist and contact-form entries and sends our emails.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the form-processing backend, provides the Turnstile CAPTCHA, and performs the request rate-limiting described above.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the static yachtmesh.io website itself.

Each of these providers processes data on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms.

Data retention

We keep waitlist and contact-form data for as long as it's useful for the purpose it was collected — for example, until early access has launched, or until a support conversation is resolved — or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time. To do so, or if you have any questions about this policy, email us at support@yachtmesh.io.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the project evolves. If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at support@yachtmesh.io.
